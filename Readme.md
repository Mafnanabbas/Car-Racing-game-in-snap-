## Implementation:

## Development Platform:

The game was developed using Snap!, a visual, block-based programming language ideal for creating interactive games. Snap! provides an intuitive environment for building games with its drag-and-drop functionality, making it suitable for implementing animations, event-driven programming, and dynamic gameplay.

## Custom Blocks:


Creating custom blocks in Snap! (Berkeley) can significantly enhance the development of a car racing game, making the programming process more efficient and organized. Custom blocks allow you to encapsulate frequently used code snippets into single commands, promoting modularity and reusability. For instance, you can create custom blocks to handle different aspects of the game, such as "Accelerate," "Decelerate," "Steer Left," and "Steer Right," each encapsulating the specific logic for controlling the car. Additionally, custom blocks can manage collision detection, lap counting, and power-up mechanics, ensuring that the game's core functionalities are neatly separated and easy to modify or expand. By using custom blocks, you can streamline the game development process, improve code readability, and facilitate troubleshooting, ultimately creating a more dynamic and engaging car racing game.

## Menu Design:

The game starts with a menu screen that contains three buttons:


1. Cars: Allows players to choose from different car designs or skins to personalize their gameplay. 2. Settings: Players can adjust the game’s volume and select the difficulty level (e.g., Easy, Medium, Hard). 3. Start: Begins the game, accompanied by a brief loading animation that builds

anticipation.

## Core Gameplay Mechanics

- \- Stationary Car Control: The player-s car remains in the center of the screen while cars approach from the opposite side.

- \- Dodging: Players must move the car left or right to avoid collisions.

- \- Jumping Mechanic: Players can jump over incoming cars. Each jump has a cooldown of 15 seconds to ensure balanced gameplay.

## Powerups:

Powerups appear periodically during gameplay and provide specific advantages:

1. Jump Powerup: Adds +5 jumps to the player’s jump count. 2. Score Powerup: Instantly increases the player’s score by +500 points. 3. Shield Powerup: Grants the player a shield for 10 seconds, allowing them to

avoid collisions.

## Scoring System:

The scoring system is tied to the difficulty level: - Higher difficulty levels increase the score at a faster rate. - Players are rewarded for their skill and endurance, encouraging them to take on greater challenges.

## Collision Handling:
